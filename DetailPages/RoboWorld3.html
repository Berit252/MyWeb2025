Robo World 3 - Ramadan do what qry?

This is a visual novel set in a fictional machine society that has inherited fragments of human language, belief, and social order. In this world, certain concepts, meanings of words, and logic are reinterpreted. The Robos thus inherit a part of the human world.

On this day called 'Ramadan Day', players will encounter several conversations between Robos and have the ability to influence the development of the Robos' stories at 'night'. Through this interactive method, players are able to glimpse a corner of this machine society.



这是一个设定在虚构的机械社会中的视觉小说，这个社会继承了人类语言、信仰和社会秩序的碎片。在这个世界里，机器人因此继承了人类世界的一部分，但某些概念、词义和逻辑都被重新诠释。

在这个被称作“Ramadan Day”的当天，玩家会接触到几段Robo之间的对话，且有权力影响Robo们‘夜晚’的故事发展。通过这种互动方式，玩家得以窥见这个机器社会的一角。
